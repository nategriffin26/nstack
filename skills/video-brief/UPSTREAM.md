License in `LICENSE`.

Local changes: `find_refs.py`'s contact-sheet tiles skip their drawn numbers when ffmpeg has no `drawtext` filter (Homebrew's ffmpeg on this Mac has none, and the unchanged script crashed with `FileNotFoundError` on every `channel … <dir>` and `search … <dir>`); the sheet's JSON line then says `"numbered": false`, and tiles stay in `n` order, row-major. Nothing else changed. Stewdio's file addressing (`in stewdio, <file>`), its standing defaults (white background, about 5 s loops, sequences under 15–20 s) and its prompt templates are not used here.

Ideas taken without copying files: real product material before any direction, then three directions as keyframes (https://github.com/alchaincyf/huashu-motion, `skills/huashu-motion/SKILL.md`); "The builder never grades its own work" and fresh critics on the render (https://github.com/echris6/motion-video-kit, MIT, `business-motion-film/SKILL.md`); every adjective becomes a number in the spec, snapshot review before the render (https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes, `skills/hyperframes/SKILL.md`); believable product UI, "Three to five beats and a payoff, 10 to 16 seconds" (https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills, `stageshow/SKILL.md`).
