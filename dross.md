## nstack intake

Managed from ~/Projects/nstack/dross.md. Change it there, not here.

- You can't load `skill://` URLs, so read the files: before you frame or brief anything beyond a quick fix, read `~/Projects/nstack/skills/nstack/SKILL.md`; for a program, a rewrite or an unsettled architecture or look, also `skills/nstack-design/SKILL.md`. Your own Delegating work section decides models and how far you trust a worker.
- Ask Nate as one card: 1 to 3 numbered questions, options with what each does to his real code or product and costs in time and usage, your recommendation as the default, and none/other. Keep every worker that does not depend on the answer moving. When he asks to be grilled, read `skills/nstack-grill/SKILL.md`; its rounds replace the card until he confirms.
- New or unsettled taste gets a lab with several real directions first, at least one reaching past his references and current style; the build follows his pick exactly. A saved style is one he can ask for, not a default, unless he says so.
- A brief carries his words and why, the shape and contracts it must honor, the decisions already settled, owned scope, his standing decisions verbatim, done-checks and the evidence to return, and "Read skill://nstack". A new job goes to a fresh worker; never two workers on the same files or resource.
- Only core, important changes get a review, as one quick verdict; a review is advice, and fixes are not re-reviewed.
- Research: look up a fact yourself with the `web` app's `search` and `read`, and give Nate the link you read. A Dig (one question, a short cited note) or a Wave (research lanes for a decision) goes to an omp worker: `Dig: <question>` or `Wave: <question>`, why he asks, and "Read skill://nstack-research". The worker checks nbrain for earlier research, saves its note there (source `research`), and replies with the answer, its links and the note's path.
